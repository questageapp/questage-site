How it works

Three steps from lamps to a show.

01

Patch

Give every lamp a stage position — Front left, Top spot, Cyc strip. Swap a bulb later; your show stays the same.

02

Build cues

Set colour and brightness, then CAPTURE. Every look becomes a numbered cue with its own fade time.

03

Run the show

GO for the next cue, BACK to step back. BLACKOUT is on screen at all times.
