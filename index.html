Stage lighting for Philips Hue

QUE Stage

Design complicated. Operate simple.
A small, reliable lighting-control system for theatre, concerts and live performance — built on Philips Hue.

What it does

Cues

Build looks, save them as cues and run the show with GO and BACK.

Fixtures, not bulbs

Design with stage positions. Swap a lamp without rewriting your show.

Blackout

One reliable button, always available.

Local & private

Runs on your own network. No account, no cloud.

Requirements

  • Philips Hue Bridge and Hue lights on your local network
  • A Mac on the same network to run QUE Stage
  • iPad, iPhone or Mac to control the show
